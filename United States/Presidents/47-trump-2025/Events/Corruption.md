# Anti-weaponization Fund
## Trump Sues IRS
- Timeline:
	- [January 29, 2026](https://www.reuters.com/markets/us/trump-sues-irs-treasury-department-10-billion-over-tax-return-leak-2026-01-30/): Trump sues IRS and Treasury for $10 billion over the disclosure of his tax returns to the media in 2019 and 2020 saying the agencies failed to take "mandatory precautions" to prevent former IRS contractor Charles Littlejohn from leaking their tax returns to "leftist media outlets", including the New York Times and ProPublica. Prosecutors charged Littlejohn in September 2023 with leaking tax records of Trump and thousands of other wealthy Americans to the media, saying he was motivated by a political agenda. Littlejohn pleaded guilty the following month to disclosing income tax return information without authorization, and was sentenced in January 2024 to five years in prison.
	- [May 18](https://www.reuters.com/world/trump-dismisses-lawsuit-against-irs-court-filing-shows-2026-05-18/): Trump voluntarily dismisses his lawsuit against the IRS in exchange for the IRS creating a $1.8 billion "Anti-Weaponization" Fund for legal claims to be paid out to Trump allies. As part of the settlement, Trump also dropped administrative claims against the government over the FBI search of Mar-a-Lago in 2022 and the investigation into potential ties between his 2016 presidential campaign and Russia. The settlement was also later [expanded](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/trump-irs-settlement-forever-bars-audits-into-tax-claims-trump-his-family-2026-05-19/) to bar the IRS from auditing Trump, relatives or companies for past tax years.
	-  [July 13](https://www.reuters.com/world/us-judge-voids-trumps-settlement-with-irs-2026-07-13/): Miami-based U.S. District Judge Kathleen Williams excoriated Trump's personal lawyers and attorneys, finding that Trump misused court in IRS case and referred his lawyers for discipline. Williams concluded Trump, IRS were not adverse to each other in case and barred Trump from citing settlement in future proceedings, a move that could nullify the portion of the agreement barring the IRS from pursuing audits into past tax claims involving Trump or his businesses.
- **Mary Trump Case**: In June 2026, Trump [settled](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/donald-trump-settles-lawsuit-against-niece-over-publication-tax-records-2026-06-17/) a lawsuit against Mary Trump (his niece) over her leaking of his tax records to the New York Times for its Pulitzer Prize-winning 2018 probe into his finances and his alleged effort to avoid taxes. In May 2024, a state appeals court found a "substantial" legal basis for Donald Trump's confidentiality claim, but said he might deserve only "nominal damages" rather than the $100 million he sought. A judge overseeing the case dismissed Donald Trump's related claims against the Times and the reporters in 2023, and later ordered him to pay $392,639 of their legal fees.
## Lawsuit Against Fund
- Judge: District Judge Leonie Brinkema
- Timeline:
	- [May 20](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/police-officers-who-guarded-capitol-sue-block-trumps-18-billion-slush-fund-2026-05-20/): Capitol Police officer Harry Dunn and Metropolitan Police Department officer Daniel Hodges who defended the Capitol during January 6, sue to block Trump's $1.8 billion 'slush fund.'
	- [May 29](https://www.reuters.com/world/us-judge-temporarily-blocks-trumps-18-billion-weaponization-fund-2026-05-29/): U.S. District Judge Leonie Brinkema of the Eastern District of Virginia blocks Trump from "taking any further action" to set up or operate his weaponization slush fund pending further legal arguments.
	- [June 1](https://www.reuters.com/world/trumps-weaponization-fund-is-hold-sources-say-2026-06-01/): Amid Congressional Republican backlash, Trump admin temporarily halts the weaponization slush fund. The DOJ said they were following the court orders to halt the fund, despite saying they disagree with the rulings.
	- [June 2](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/with-weaponization-fund-doubt-trump-will-keep-tax-audit-immunity-sources-say-2026-06-02/): Blanche drops Trump's weaponization slush fund permanently but says agreement barring future audits into Trump's past tax records will remain in place.
	- [June 12](https://www.reuters.com/world/us-judge-indefinitely-blocks-trumps-anti-weaponization-fund-2026-06-12/): Judge Brinkema indefinitely blocks Trump's 'anti-weaponization' fund saying DOJ statements insufficient, demands sworn declaration fund won't proceed.
## Congressional Backlash
- **ICE Funding**: Senate Republicans [delay](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/republicans-flee-washington-after-flap-trumps-weaponization-fund-ballroom-2026-05-21/) ICE funding bill (May 2026) over Trump's 'anti-weaponization' fund and ballroom funds. The move came after Acting DOJ head Blanche refused to rule out using the 'anti-weaponization' fund to pay those convicted of violent crimes during January 6.
- **Blanch Confirmation**: After Trump fired Pam Bondi, Todd Blanche took over as interim DOJ head and Trump later nominated him. This led to a Congressional [confrontation](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/trump-defends-anti-weaponization-fund-blanche-nomination-stalls-2026-07-31/) over confirming Blanche due to Senate opposition to Trump's weaponization slush fund.

# Russian evidence
## DOJ indictment of 12 Russians
- July 13, 2018
- Operation:
	- Spear phished Democratic campaign employees and volunteers.
	- Stole usernames and passwords.
	- Compromised DCCC and DNC networks.
	- Installed malware to capture keystrokes and monitor computers.
	- Stole emails and other documents.
	- Created fake online identities to distribute the stolen material.
	- Used cryptocurrency and international proxy infrastructure to conceal their identities.

## Mueller investigation
- Independently documented the Russian hacking
- Concluded that GRU officers hacked the Clinton campaign, DNC, and DCCC, then distributed stolen material through DCLeaks, Guccifer 2.0, and WikiLeaks
## Senate Intelligence Committee
- multiyear investigation and published five volumes examining Russian interference.
- Findings:
	- GRU officers hacked the DNC and DCCC.
	- They compromised John Podesta's email account and other Democratic officials' accounts.
	- They stole politically sensitive material.
	- They distributed the material through GRU-controlled personas and WikiLeaks.
	- Putin directed the broader operation.

# Leakers
## Guccifer 2.0 and DCLeaks
- GRU created fake identities to distribute the stolen material.
- WikiLeaks received and published stolen material originating from the Russian operation.

## WikiLeaks
- Julian Assange repeatedly denied that the DNC material came from Russian intelligence
- He suggested that the source was an insider and made statements implying that former DNC employee Seth Rich might have been connected to the leak (became the foundation for the Seth Rich conspiracy theory)
- However, there was no reliable evidence that Rich provided the emails to WikiLeaks
- Mueller investigation examined this alternative explanation and found: Guccifer 2.0 communicated with WikiLeaks, DCLeaks communicated with WikiLeaks, WikiLeaks received stolen documents, file metadata and transfer-related evidence connected material published by WikiLeaks to material obtained during the Russian hacking operation.
- The Mueller investigation established that the GRU obtained the documents and that WikiLeaks published them. It also found evidence connecting the GRU's personas to WikiLeaks.
- The Senate Intelligence Committee subsequent 2020 report concluded that the GRU provided stolen material to WikiLeaks as part of the broader Russian hack-and-leak operation.
- January 2017 Intelligence Community Assessment also concluded, with high confidence, that the GRU used Guccifer 2.0, DCLeaks, and WikiLeaks to release information obtained through its cyber operations.

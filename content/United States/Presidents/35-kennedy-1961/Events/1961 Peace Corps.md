# Overview
- Purpose is to assist developing countries by providing skilled workers in education, agriculture, health (particular focus on combating HIV/AIDS), trade, technology, environmental protection, women’s economic empowerment, and community development. 
- Popular among college students

# Agency
## Establishment
- Established by Kennedy executive order [10924](https://archives.federalregister.gov/issue_slice/1961/3/2/1787-1797.pdf) signed on March 1, 1961
- Authorized by Congress on September 22, 1961 under the Peace Corps Act ([Public Law 87-293, 75 Stat. 612](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/STATUTE-75/pdf/STATUTE-75-Pg612.pdf))
## Structure
- From 1971 to 1981 it was a subagency of an independent agency called ACTION
### Volunteers
- Assigned to specific projects based on their skills, education, and experience. 
- Once abroad, the volunteer is expected to function for two years as a good neighbor in the host country, to speak its language, and to live on a level comparable to that of the volunteer’s counterpart.
### Leadership
- First director of the Peace Corps was Kennedy’s brother-in-law R. Sargent Shriver.

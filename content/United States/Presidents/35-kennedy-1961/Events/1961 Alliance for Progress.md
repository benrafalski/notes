# Overview
- International economic development program created by the US for 22 Latin American countries
- Established in August 1961 under the Charter of Punta del Este (Uruguay)
- Basically a Marshal Plan for Latin America
- Also called *Alianza*
- [Proposal speech](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cgaciM1g2QE)


# Background
- **Eisenhower policy (New Look)**
	- After end of Korean War in 1953 Eisenhower shifted to Latin America
	- CIA used "New Look" to undermine governments the US saw as 'threatening regional stability'
	- Many Latin Americans saw this as the US merely supporting pro-US elites ans US economic interests
	- Often led to the US supporting anti-communist btu also anti-democratic regimes with military/economic aid
- Covert Operations
	- Guatemala: CIA supported and armed coup against the Soviet leaning Jacobo Arbenz government

# Objectives
## Security
- Maintain US dominance in western hemisphere
- Avoid another Cuba
- Draw Latin American nations away from Soviets
- US still supported covert CIA operations under Kennedy, such as the Bay of Pigs
## Development
Eisenhower was mostly security due to a slowing US economy at the time making aid difficult, Kennedy brought in a bunch of New Dealers who felt development was also needed. They wanted to make liberal democratic societies in Latin American that mirrored what the US had. This policy failed as military dictatorships and failed governments rose during the 1960s
### Goals
- Democratic government 
- Economic and social development
- Sustained growth in per capita income
- More equitable distribution of income
- Accelerated development in industry/agriculture
- Agrarian reform
- Improvement of health and welfare
- Stabilization of export prices 
- Domestic price stability
# Funding
- Seed funding
	- Estimated $20 billion needed in first 10 years
	- Half from the United States and the rest from international lending agencies and private sources.
- Inter-American Committee on the Alliance for Progress (CIAP): created in 1963 to serve as the coordinating agent between the international community and the Latin American countries involved
- Aid to Latin American as % of total US foreign aid: 2-3% under Truman, 5% under Eisenhower, 18% under Kennedy

# Legacy
- Failure: some new schools, hospitals, and other physical plants but massive land reform was not achieved and population more than kept pace with gains in health and welfare. 
- Reason: U.S. aid decreased over the years, and political tensions between the United States and Latin America increased.
- Dissolution: dissolved in 1973 by the Organization of American States

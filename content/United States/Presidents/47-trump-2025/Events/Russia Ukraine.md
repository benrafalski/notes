## Diesel Strikes
In September 2026, Trump called on Ukrainian President Zelenskiy to [stop targeting Russian diesel infrastructure](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/trump-tells-ukraines-zelenskiy-stop-hitting-russian-diesel-2026-09-13/), saying the attacks were causing a shortage of the ​fuel that is "hurting the world". Trump's comments came when U.S. national average diesel price hit record high due to Trump's Iran war. A wave of long-distance Ukrainian ​drone attacks on Russian oil refineries in recent months  reduced Russia's fuel production, triggering gasoline shortages ​across the country. Ukraine, which faces regular Russian attacks on its own ​energy infrastructure, says refineries are legitimate military targets.

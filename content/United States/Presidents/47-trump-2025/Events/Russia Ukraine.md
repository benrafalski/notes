## Diesel
- In September 2026, Trump called on Ukrainian President Zelenskiy to [stop targeting Russian diesel infrastructure](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/trump-tells-ukraines-zelenskiy-stop-hitting-russian-diesel-2026-09-13/), saying the attacks were causing a shortage of the ​fuel that is "hurting the world". Trump's comments came when U.S. national average diesel price hit record high due to Trump's Iran war. A wave of long-distance Ukrainian ​drone attacks on Russian oil refineries in recent months  reduced Russia's fuel production, triggering gasoline shortages ​across the country. Ukraine, which faces regular Russian attacks on its own ​energy infrastructure, says refineries are legitimate military targets.
- In October, Russia agreed to supply[ 300,000 tons of diesel](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/trump-big-announcement-coming-up-diesel-2026-10-09/) now and more later to the US, as diesel prices hit $6.28 a gallon ahead of the November midterms. Zelensky responded saying a gift to Putin would not bring peace. The diesel exports could provide Moscow with billions of dollars ​in revenues that could help Putin continue the war with Ukraine.

# Sanctions
- **Lindsey Graham Sanctions** (September 2026): Congress passes [sweeping sanctions and tariff bill](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/us-house-takes-up-russia-sanctions-bill-backed-by-late-senator-graham-2026-09-16/) intended to increase economic pressure on Russia. The bill was introduced 1-1/2 years before by the late Senator Lindsey Graham. House passed 262 to 159, Senate passed 86 to 11. It faced pushback because it also authorizes Trump to impose stiff tariffs of up to 100% on China, India and other countries to reduce their dependence ​on Russian oil and gas, as well as to extend sanctions on Iran. However in October, Trump [waved the Lindsey Graham Act sanctions and tariffs](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/rubio-says-trump-take-up-60-days-weigh-russia-sanctions-2026-10-18/) on Russia and opted for a 60 day review. The move came right before midterms and only a week after the US agreed to buy Russian diesel with US diesel prices still near record highs.

# Israel
## Trump Peace Plan
- **Hamas Disarmament**: In July 2026, Trump [said](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/israeli-strikes-kill-three-gaza-including-two-children-amid-new-ceasefire-push-2026-07-30/) a deal was reached for phased disarmament of Hamas, despite Israel earlier saying they had rejected the proposal and Hamas saying they would only follow through with the "draft agreement" if Israel fully withdrew from Gaza. Hamas avoids the use of the term "disarmament" and said the group agreed to hand over weapons to be stored under a U.S.-backed Palestinian technocrat administration that would run the affairs in ​Gaza under the supervision of Trump's Board of Peace.
- **Israel Rejection**: In August 2026, Netanyahu said he [rejects](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/israel-rejects-trumps-15-point-plan-gaza-pm-netanyahu-says-2026-08-09/) Trump's 15-point Gaza plan for disarmament. Netanyahu is struggling in the polls as he seeks re-election at a vote due on October 27 but is caught between far-right ministers angered by concessions in Gaza and his key military backer, Trump, who wants progress toward ending the conflict in Gaza. Over the previous week, Israel had halted it's attacks in Gaza with sources saying Israel's position now was that it had agreed that the military would only act against what it perceives as immediate threats in Gaza ​and no longer carry out targeted assassinations as it had been doing on a near-daily basis.
- **US Funds**: In August 2026, the Trump admin sent the first funds, totaling [$200 million](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/us-provide-206-million-gaza-peacekeeping-force-letters-show-2026-08-19/), to the International Stabilization Force that was part of the U.S.-sponsored Gaza peace deal.

# Syria
## Terrorist Sponsors List
In August 2026, the US r[emoved Syria from its list of state sponsors of terrorism](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/us-removes-syrias-designation-state-sponsor-terrorism-2026-08-24/), lifting what Damascus says was the last major impediment to investing in the country as Washington deepens ties to ​the new Syrian government. The designation carries restrictions on U.S. foreign assistance, defense exports and sales, certain dual-use items and financial transactions, and had remained a major deterrent for banks and investors even after Washington dismantled its broader sanctions program on ​Syria.

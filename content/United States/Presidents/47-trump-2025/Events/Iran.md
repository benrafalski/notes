
# Iran War
## July 
- [July 25](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/trump-vows-punish-iran-houthis-attacks-red-sea-2026-07-24/):  Houthis fire on Saudi oil installations and US halts strikes in Iran for first night in two ‌weeks after Trump was advised that the campaign ran its course.
- [July 29](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/us-saudis-attack-iran-backed-groups-iraq-threatening-intensify-conflict-2026-07-29/): US resumes strikes with US-Saudi strikes hitting Iran-backed targets in Iraq. Tehran also rejects Oman proposal for regional management of Hormuz as they continue targeting ships navigating the strait.
- [July 31](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/trump-says-iran-not-blame-minnesota-cyber-attack-2026-07-31/): Trump says Iran not to blame for Minnesota water systems cyberattacks, instead blames "it on Minnesota because they're grossly incompetent." Trump's comments were at odds with Minnesota Fusion Center (state-level intelligence entity) and US officials who all found the July 26 and 27 hacks were likely Tehran.
## August
- [August 3](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/trump-says-iran-talks-take-place-monday-sets-no-deadline-deal-2026-08-02/): Iran says there were no talks under ​way with the US and no plans for any meetings, contradicting Trump who had ‌cited talks he said would take place that afternoon as justification for calling off attacks after previously announcing plans for "massive attacks."

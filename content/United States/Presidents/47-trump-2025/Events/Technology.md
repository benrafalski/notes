# Crypto
## Clarity Act
In September 2026, the procedural vote on Clarity Act fell short of the 60 votes it needed. The failure came despite the crypto industry spending roughly [$300 million](https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/crypto-bills-defeat-shows-limits-industrys-political-machine-2026-09-16/) on 2026 and 2024 elections. In June, Trump reported more than $1.4 billion in income from his family's crypto ventures, stiffening Democrats' resolve to push for tougher language on officeholder restrictions. The White House ultimately agreed to several restrictions but Democrats said they didn't go far enough.

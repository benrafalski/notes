# Trans Issues
## Trans Minors
- **Federal Healthcare**: In August 2026, Trump admin [new rule](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/us-bars-federal-funding-gender-affirming-care-low-income-minors-2026-08-12/) bars Medicaid and CHIP from funding puberty blockers, hormone therapy and surgeries to treat gender dysphoria ​in minors.
## Trans prisoners
- In October 2026 the [Supreme Court rejected](https://www.reuters.com/world/supreme-court-wont-allow-trump-administration-restrictions-transgender-inmates-2026-10-21/) a bid by Trump to immediately enforce One of his policies issued in February that prohibits medical and surgical treatments as well as any "social accommodations" such as clothing and hair removal devices that could be used to alter a person's appearance to align with their gender identity. Washington, DC based US District Judge Royce Lamberth previously blocked Trump's restrictions on federal prisoners in June, concluding that the policy was likely "arbitrary and capricious" in violation of the APA. In September, the US Court of Appeals for the District of Columbia also refuse to lift Lamberth's ruling.
## University of Pennsylvania
- On Mar 19, 2025, the Trump admin [suspended $175 million](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/us-suspends-funding-university-pennsylvania-over-transgender-policy-2025-03-19/) in funding to the University of Pennsylvania over its transgender sports policies. UPenn previously made national headlines in 2022 when a transgender student athlete, swimmer Lia Thomas, competed on its women's swim team. 
- On April 28, the [Trump admin demanded](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/us-says-upenns-transgender-policy-illegal-demands-return-athletic-awards-2025-04-28/), within 10 days, that UPenn issue a statement saying it will "comply with federal law", transfer records and awards won by transgender athletes to athletes assigned female at birth, and issue a letter of apology to the female athletes. 
- On July 1, UPenn agreed with Trump [to disavow and apologize](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/university-pennsylvania-reaches-compliance-deal-with-trump-administration-2025-07-02/) for having allowed transgender women to compete in female-only sports and to erase any athletic records and titles they earned.
## Trans passport policy
- On January 20, 2025, Trump signed an EO directing the government to [recognize only two biologically distinct sexes](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/trump-passport-policy-discriminates-against-transgender-people-judge-rules-2025-04-18/), male and female. The order also directed the State Department to change its policies to only issue passports that "accurately reflect the holder's sex." On April 18, US District Judge Julia Kobick in Boston [issued a preliminary injunction](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/us-judge-blocks-trump-passport-policy-targeting-transgender-people-2025-06-17/), that ordered Trump to allow six trans plaintiffs to obtain passports consistent with their gender identities. Kobick declined to make the injunction nationwide. Kobick said Trump's policy of refusing to issue passports to transgender and nonbinary Americans that reflect their gender identities is likely unconstitutional. Biden's admin previously allowed an 'X' gender marker on passports in 2022. On June 17 Kobick made her injunction nationwide after granting the case class action status.
- On September 4, the Boston-based 1st U.S. Circuit Court of Appeals [declined Trump's bid](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/us-appeals-court-rejects-trump-bid-restore-passport-policy-targeting-transgender-2025-09-04/) to put on hold Kobick's injunction. 
- November 6: Supreme Court rules 6-3 in shadow docket ruling [to stay Kobick's injunction](https://www.reuters.com/world/us-supreme-court-allows-trumps-passport-policy-targeting-transgender-people-2025-11-06/), allowing Trump to enforce its passport policy while the legal challenge continues.
## Trans Sports
- On July 22, the United States Olympic & Paralympic Committee (USOPC) [updated its policy](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/us-olympic-paralympic-committee-bars-transgender-women-competing-womens-sports-2025-07-22/) to align with Trump's executive order barring transgender women from competing in women's sports. 
- On August 4, the USCIS updated its immigration policy to [restrict visa eligibility for transgender women](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/us-government-restricts-sports-visas-transgender-women-2025-08-04/) seeking to compete in women's sports.
## Trans Military Ban
- On August 7, U.S. Air Force [denied early retirement to all transgender service members](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/us-air-force-denies-early-retirement-group-transgender-service-members-2025-08-07/) with between 15 and 18 years of military service, opting instead to force them out with no retirement benefits. The Air Force's decision follows a policy detailed in a May 23 memo, which stated that airmen with 15-18 years of service could request early retirement following Trump's trans military ban. The Air Force noted that it approved early retirement for more senior members who self-identified as transgender and had 18-20 years of service (regular retirement happens after 20 years).
- November 24: DOJ judicial misconduct complaint against District Judge Reyes, who oversaw the trans military ban lawsuit, [is dismissed](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/us-dojs-misconduct-complaint-against-judge-transgender-military-ban-case-gets-2025-11-25/) by Chief U.S. Circuit Judge Sri Srinivasan. 
- June 1, 2026: U.S. Court of Appeals for the District of Columbia Circuit rules 2-1 that [Trump's trans military ban is discriminatory](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/us-court-blocks-pentagon-removing-transgender-troops-now-2026-06-01/) and that current service members can't be fired but allowed the block on enlisting to remain pending outcome of lawsuit.
## Gender Ideology Ban
- October 20: U.S. District Judge Ann Aiken issues injunction [blocking Trump from withholding $35 million](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/us-judge-block-trump-directive-cut-gender-ideology-states-sex-ed-materials-2025-10-20/) in funding for the Personal Responsibility Education Program (PREP) and Title V Sexual Risk Avoidance Education programs to Democratic states over their refusal to remove all references to "gender ideology" from sexual health education curricula.
## Trans Kids
- March 19, 2026: U.S. District Judge Mustafa Kasubhai in Eugene, Oregon [bars RFK Jr. from efforts](https://www.reuters.com/business/healthcare-pharmaceuticals/judge-says-he-will-bar-rfk-move-aimed-cutting-gender-affirming-care-minors-2026-03-20/) to restricted medical providers from providing gender affirming care to minors saying Kennedy overstepped his authority and violated federal law. Kennedy issued a declaration in December saying HHS could bar healthcare providers that offer gender-affirming care to children from Medicaid and Medicare and prohibit the Children's Health Insurance Program from paying for it.
- May 13: District Judge Mary McElroy rules that Rhode Island Hospital, run by Brown Health, [did not have to comply](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/us-judge-blocks-justice-department-bid-rhode-island-hospital-transgender-care-2026-05-14/) with a sweeping DOJ subpoena seeking a wide range of documents, including medical records of minors treated for gender dysphoria with drugs such as puberty blockers. McElroy rejected DOJ claims that it was investigating alleged off-label use of FDA-approved drugs, and said enforcing the subpoena would violate the privacy rights of minor patients.
## Trans Schools
- April 6, 2026: U.S. Education Department [terminates six resolution agreements](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/trump-administration-ends-some-civil-rights-settlements-backing-transgender-2026-04-06/) reached through what it called the manipulation of Title IX under previous presidents. The agreements included deals reached with the Sacramento City Unified School District in California, the Cape Henlopen School District in Delaware, the Fife School District in Washington state, the Delaware Valley School District in Pennsylvania, the La Mesa-Spring Valley School District in California and Taft College in California.
## FTC Trans Probe
- May 7, 2026: U.S. District Judge James Boasberg grants preliminary injunction, [blocking the FTC from demanding internal communications and financial records](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/us-ftcs-investigation-trans-youth-care-was-retaliatory-judge-says-2026-05-08/) from the Endocrine Society and World Professional Association for Transgender Health organizations, saying the FTC's investigation of trans youth care was 'retaliatory.'
## Trans Medical Records
- June 24, 2026: U.S. District Judge Katherine Polk Failla in Manhattan issues TRO [blocking Trump DOJ from using grand jury subpoenas to obtain medical records of transgender patients](https://www.reuters.com/world/us-judge-block-doj-obtaining-transgender-care-records-new-york-hospitals-2026-06-24/) who as minors received gender-affirming care from New York City healthcare providers. The subpoena was issued by a grand jury in the Northern District of Texas, a venue long popular among conservative litigants and that had recently emerged as a hub for the DOJ after numerous judges nationally quashed some of the 20-plus administrative subpoenas it had previously issued to doctors and clinics providing such care in July 2025.

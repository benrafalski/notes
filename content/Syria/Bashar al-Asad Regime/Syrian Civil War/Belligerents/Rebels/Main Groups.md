# FSA
Lifetime:
- Emerges: July 2011
- Peak: 2012–early 2013
- Decline: 2013–2015 (fragmentation)
- Status: Brand absorbed into SNA
Geographical Control:
- Early: Rural Homs, Daraa, Idlib, Aleppo countryside
Notes:
- Not a unified army, label used by thousands of semi-independent brigades
- Militarily eclipsed by Islamist groups

# Jabhat al-Nusra → Jabhat Fateh al-Sham → HTS
Lifetime:
- Emerges: Jan 2012 (as al-Nusra)
- Peak: 2015–2020
- Still Active: Yes (as HTS)
Geographical Control:
- 2013–15: Parts of Idlib, Aleppo, Hama
- 2015–2018: Core of Army of Conquest
- 2019–present: Most of Idlib province
Notes:
- Best-organized rebel force
- Transitioned from jihadist insurgency → proto-state actor

# Ahrar al-Sham
Lifetime:
- Emerges: 2011–2012
- Peak: 2013–2015
- Decline: 2016–2018 (splits, HTS pressure)
Geographical Control:
- Idlib province
- Northern Hama
- Parts of Aleppo countryside
Notes:
- Islamist but Syrian-nationalist
- Major Nusra/HTS rival
- Eventually absorbed or sidelined
# Islamic Front \[Coalition\]
Lifetime:
- Forms: Nov 2013
	- Members: Ahrar al-Sham, Jaysh al-Islam, Suqour al-Sham, Liwa al-Tawhid
- Peak: 2014
- Dissolves: 2015
Geographical Control:
- Large swaths of north & Damascus suburbs
Notes:
- Attempt to create a unified Islamist alternative to FSA
- Fell apart due to leadership deaths and battlefield losses

# Jaysh al-Islam
Lifetime:
- Emerges: 2012
- Peak: 2013–2015
- Destroyed: 2018 (Eastern Ghouta falls)
Geographical Control:
- Eastern Ghouta (near Damascus)
Notes:
- Extremely centralized command
- Saudi-backed

# Army of Conquest (Jaish al-Fatah) \[Coalition\]
Lifetime:
- Forms: 2015
	- Members: al-Nusra, Ahrar al-Sham, smaller Islamist factions
- Peak: 2015–early 2016
- Dissolves: 2017
Geographical Control:
- Idlib city (2015 capture)
- Jisr al-Shughur
- Parts of Latakia countryside
Notes:
- Most successful rebel coalition ever
- Russian intervention killed momentum
# Levant Front (al-Jabha al-Shamiya)
Lifetime:
- Emerges: 2014
- Peak: 2016–2018
- Still Active: Yes (SNA)
Geographical Control:
- Northern Aleppo
- Azaz, Mare’, Afrin (post-2018)
Notes:
- Hybrid Islamist/FSA group
- Key Turkish proxy

# Syrian Revolutionaries Front (SRF)
Lifetime:
- Emerges: 2013
- Peak: 2014
- Destroyed: 2015 (defeated by al-Nusra)
Geographical Control:
- Idlib, Hama countryside
Notes:
- CIA-backed FSA coalition
- Collapsed after jihadist infighting

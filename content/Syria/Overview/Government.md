# Mukhabarat
## Overview
- Regime structure:
	- Layer 1 (President): everything served the Assad family
	- Layer 2 (National Security Bureau): above security agencies and helped the Assad manage them
	- Layer 3 (Mukhabarat Agencies):
		- General Intelligence Directorate (GID)
		- Military Intelligence Directorate (MID)
		- Air Force Intelligence Directorate (AFI)
		- Political Security Directorate (PSD)
## Major Agencies
### General Intelligence Directorate (GID)
Functions
- Domestic surveillance
- Counterintelligence
- Foreign intelligence
- Monitoring dissidents
- Monitoring political opposition
- Running informants
Important branches:
- Branch 251 (Internal Security)
- Branch 285 (External Security)
Leaders:
- Ali Mamlouk (2005-12): later promoted to head of the National Security Bureau and became one of Assad's closest advisers
- Mohammed Dib Zaitoun: succeeded Mamlouk
